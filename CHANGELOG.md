# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-29

🧸 First release: a safe keyboard playground for toddlers, with Boo the teddy.

- **One shortcut in, one shortcut out**: ⌃⌥⌘K from any app starts play, and pressing it again
  goes straight back to work. Opening the app starts play too. Hold esc for 3 seconds and answer
  a sum (11–18) as the other way out.
- **Locks the screen for play**: full screen on every display; the Dock, menu bar, ⌘Tab, Force
  Quit and sleep are blocked. The optional strong lock (Accessibility) catches every other key,
  media key and trackpad swipe.
- **Letters**: a big letter, a picture word and a slow, clear voice ("B is for ball!"), with
  several words per letter taking turns and animal sounds.
- **Numbers**: things pop in one at a time in step with the counting voice.
- **Every key does something**: colours and shapes, bubbles (Space), a rainbow (Return), peekaboo
  (Tab), whoosh (Delete), Boo hops and jumps (arrows) and claps (modifier keys). Mashing gives a
  confetti burst. Things never cover each other or Boo.
- **Family peekaboo**: photos with names and your own recorded voices, kept only on your Mac.
- **Gentle ending** after a set play time, **calm mode**, **day / night** skies, a choice of
  voice and speed, mouse taps and finger painting.
- Settings window with Play, Sound, Family, Safety and General tabs; Appearance System / Light /
  Dark; open at login; 🧸 menu-bar menu.
